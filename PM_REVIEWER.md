# 👔 Project Manager’s Defense Reviewer

This guide is for the Project Manager to handle "management" and "vision" questions during the defense.

---

## 1. The "Big Picture" Questions

**Q: "Why did you choose an E-Grocery app?"**
*   **Answer:** "We wanted to build a simple e-commerce solution for small grocery stores. Our goal was to create a functional platform that handles the essential needs of a small business, like inventory management and customer ordering."

**Q: "How would you describe the quality and design of the app?"**
*   **Answer:** "We focused on building a **Minimum Viable Product (MVP)**. Our priority was to ensure that the core 'Data Journey'—from product creation to customer checkout—works reliably. We kept the design simple to ensure the app remains lightweight and easy to navigate."

---

## 2. Team & Management Questions

**Q: "How did you divide the tasks in your group?"**
*   **Answer:** "We used a functional approach. We had members focusing on the Database logic, others on the UI using Tailwind CSS, and I managed the project scope to ensure we delivered all the core features on time."

**Q: "What was the biggest challenge you faced as a team?"**
*   **Answer:** "The biggest challenge was ensuring the logic between the cart and the inventory was synchronized. We solved this through careful testing of our 'Place Order' loop to make sure stock levels were updating correctly."

---

## 3. Strategy & Decisions

**Q: "Why did you use ASP.NET MVC?"**
*   **Answer:** "MVC allowed us to keep the project organized. It separates the business logic from the user interface, which made it easier for us to collaborate and debug issues as a team."

**Q: "If you had more time, what would you improve?"**
*   **Answer:** "We would enhance the UI with more interactive elements and add a more detailed order tracking system for the customers."

---

## 4. Problem Solving

**Q: "How did you handle the project timeline?"**
*   **Answer:** "We focused on a **'Core First'** strategy. We prioritized the login, product listing, and checkout systems. This ensured we had a fully working prototype even with a limited timeframe."

---

## 💡 3 Golden Rules for the PM during Defense:

1.  **Focus on Functionality:** If they critique the design, pivot to the logic: *"Our focus was ensuring the database operations and inventory calculations were 100% accurate."*
2.  **Be Honest but Professional:** It’s okay to say it's an MVP. It shows you understand the development lifecycle.
3.  **Acknowledge Learning:** If they find a bug, say: *"That is a great observation. It's something we've noted for the next version of our development."*
